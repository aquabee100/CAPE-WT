# Data access

The primary nine-turbine SCADA archive and event information cannot be redistributed. Authorised users should place the archive at:

`src/wt_cm/data/MANF2017-2022.pkl`

Its frozen checksum is in `docs/protocol/RAW_INPUT_SHA256.txt`.

The independent Kelmarsh data are public:

Plumley, C. (2022), *Kelmarsh Wind Farm Data*. Zenodo: `10.5281/zenodo.7212475`.
